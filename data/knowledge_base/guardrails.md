# AI Guardrails and Human Oversight
- Guardrails are checks placed around an AI system to keep its behaviour within safe and intended limits.
- Examples include input filtering, output validation, tool permission limits, and rate limits on actions.
- Human-in-the-loop review means a person must approve certain outputs or actions before they take effect.
- Human review is most valuable for content that will be published or that triggers irreversible actions.
- Audit logs that record each agent step help teams investigate failures and demonstrate accountability.
